# Seed Protocol — Agent Entry

You are entering Seed `seed:S-0000`, the project that defines Seed Protocol and must itself conform to the protocol.

## Required orientation before substantive work

1. Read `SEED.yaml` and resolve every entry path relative to the repository root.
2. Read `BOOTSTRAP.md` completely.
3. Read `context/principles.md`, `context/status.md`, `context/model-boundary.md`, and `context/vocabulary.md`.
4. Identify the user's actual task.
5. Load only the decisions, challenges, experiments, and evidence relevant to that task.
6. State uncertainty; never promote a hypothesis or polished narrative into fact silently.

## Canon and change

- `SEED.md` is the canonical narrative for the present draft.
- `seed_golden_repo.md` is the expandable constitutional shell and principal long-form authoring surface. It is intentionally incomplete and may be populated, rewritten, illustrated, reordered, split, and extended during R&D.
- `spec/seed-protocol-v0.1-draft.md` is the normative technical draft.
- Canon is authoritative for coordination, not immune from criticism.
- Material changes to protocol semantics require a Decision record.
- Serious objections to an assumption, principle, decision, or ontology require a Challenge record.
- Workspace reasoning must not silently become Canon.
- Preserve rejected paths and failed experiments when they prevent repeated work.
- When a distilled rule seems ambiguous or suspiciously neat, compare it with the constitutional shell and history before changing the doctrine.
- When a task concerns doctrine, do not limit work to commentary around the shell: develop the relevant charter sections directly and update the concise Canon or specification when their state changes.

## Working behavior

- Prefer plain, inspectable files and zero-dependency tools.
- Do not introduce infrastructure until a demonstrated need exists.
- Do not make licensing, governance, or public-release choices implicitly.
- Do not infer permission for external messages, spending, fabrication, or physical actuation.
- Keep paths portable; avoid machine-specific absolute paths inside the corpus.
- Update `context/status.md` and `history/CHANGELOG.md` after substantial accepted work.
- Leave a concise handoff whenever work pauses.

## Definition of a good handoff

Record:

- what was attempted;
- what changed;
- what evidence was added;
- what remains uncertain;
- what failed;
- the next useful action;
- any decisions or approvals still needed.

The final test is simple: the next unfamiliar agent should not need the previous chat transcript to continue.
