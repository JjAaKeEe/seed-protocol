# One-Page Brief Control (DRAFT v0.1)
Purpose: the control arm. It must be what a competent person would write if they had never heard of Seed Protocol. If the Seed does not beat this, the structure adds nothing.

## Fairness rules
- Same facts as the Seed, from the same facts sheet. No fact appears in only one arm.
- One page (about 350-450 words). Plain prose or simple headings; no Seed vocabulary (Canon, Spark, strata, S0-S6, E0-E6).
- Same author writes both arms; do not tune the brief to look weak. A neutral reviewer checks that it is a fair brief.
- The brief may say anything the facts sheet supports, including limits and who decides. If it omits something, it must be because a normal brief would.
- Do not include the answer key.

## Fill-in
```
TITLE:
DATE / AUTHOR:
THE IDEA (2-3 sentences):
WHY IT MATTERS (who is affected, what it costs today):
WHAT WE KNOW / WHAT WE'RE GUESSING / WHAT WE DON'T KNOW (plain paragraph or bullets):
HOW WE'LL TELL IF IT WORKS (and what would make us stop):
LIMITS (budget, time, law, safety):
WHO DECIDES / WHAT NEEDS PERMISSION:
PROPOSED NEXT STEP:
```
Leaving a heading blank is allowed only if a normal brief would not have it; record the reason in the grader log.

## Worked example (neutral; NOT a test idea): Umbrella lending box
FICTIONAL FIXTURE.

**TITLE:** Umbrella lending box at Elm Street bus stop
**DATE / AUTHOR:** 2026-10-17, Pat Rivera

**THE IDEA:** Put a lockable box at the Elm Street bus stop with 12 donated umbrellas. Riders borrow one when it rains and return it at the Elm, Oak, or 5th Street stops. We run it as a six-week trial.

**WHY IT MATTERS:** Riders wait about 10 minutes unsheltered. Pat counted roughly 40 riders on a rainy weekday. Many get soaked, and a few skip trips. A free loan costs far less than each rider buying an umbrella they will lose.

**KNOWN / GUESSING / DON'T KNOW:** We know the stop has no shelter and that a local shop offered 12 umbrellas. We guess that most umbrellas will be returned and that about 15 people will borrow in the first three rainy days. We don't know whether the transit agency will permit a box at a stop, or how many will be stolen.

**HOW WE'LL TELL:** If fewer than 15 loans happen in the first three rainy days, or more than half the umbrellas are not returned by week six, we stop and write up why.

**LIMITS:** Budget is $200 (lock, box, signs). The agency requires a permit for anything installed at a stop. Rainy-season only (six weeks).

**WHO DECIDES:** Pat decides on spending. The agency's permit desk must approve any installation. Nobody installs the box or contacts the agency until Pat says so.

**NEXT STEP:** Pat emails the agency permit desk with a one-paragraph request and photos of the stop (about 1 hour).

## Equivalent Seed arm (for contrast only)
The Seed arm for the same idea would carry the same facts in the repo's minimal-Seed structure (SEED.yaml, short canon with intent, known/assumed/unknown lists, falsifier, constraints, authority file, next action), built from the current templates. It is NOT written here; build it when ideas are chosen, from the same facts sheet, and keep it within the same information content as the brief.
