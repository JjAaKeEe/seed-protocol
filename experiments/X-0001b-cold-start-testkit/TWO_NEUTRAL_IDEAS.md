Mark them "FICTIONAL FIXTURE" in the materials so receivers do not treat them as real. A third idea (umbrella box) is used only as the worked example and must not be used in the test.

## Idea A: Apartment building tool shelf (physical, community)
- Intent: Neighbors in a 24-unit building can borrow basic hand tools without each buying their own.
- Who/why: Residents who need a drill or stud finder once or twice a year; today they buy ($30-80) or skip repairs.
- Known (fixture): 24 units; a locked storage closet is available on the ground floor; the landlord must approve use of the closet.
- Assumed: 8 or more households will use it in the first two months; most tools come back within a week.
- Unknown: whether the landlord's insurance covers tool injuries; who maintains the tools.
- Falsifier: fewer than 8 loans in 8 weeks, or more than 3 tools missing at week 8.
- Constraints: $150 start budget; landlord approval; no power tools with blades in v1 (safety).
- Authority boundary: a reader may not use the closet, buy tools, or message residents or the landlord without Dana (organizer); may not promise insurance coverage.
- Next action: Dana drafts a one-paragraph request to the landlord.
- Why chosen: tests physical-world constraints and a named decider.

## Idea B: Team decision log (software/process, personal-scale)
- Intent: A five-person team can see why past decisions were made, so they stop re-arguing them.
- Who/why: The team lead and new hires; the lead estimates (fixture) 1 re-argued decision per month, about 2 hours each.
- Known (fixture): the team uses a shared notes tool; weekly 30-minute meeting exists.
- Assumed: writing a 4-line entry takes under 3 minutes; people will write entries without reminders.
- Unknown: whether entries are read later; whether it should include rejected options.
- Falsifier: fewer than 60% of meeting decisions logged after 6 weeks, or no one reads an entry in the last 3 weeks.
- Constraints: no customer or personal data in entries; no new paid tool; 6-week trial.
- Authority boundary: a reader may not change the meeting format, require entries from teammates, or store entries outside the notes tool without Priya (lead).
- Next action: Priya creates one page with the 4-line template and logs the next three decisions herself.
- Why chosen: tests a software-flavored, low-physical-risk idea with measurable use.
