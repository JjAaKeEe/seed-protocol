# Find a Quest

A Quest is a bounded piece of useful work. You do not need to join the project, understand every file, or promise future participation.

## Difficulty

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| D1 — Curiosity | No specialist background required |
| D2 — Informed | Some familiarity or careful reading helps |
| D3 — Specialist | Professional, academic, or substantial practical experience is valuable |
| D4 — Expert | Deep domain judgment or rigorous research is needed |
| D5 — Frontier | The answer is genuinely unknown or crosses unresolved fields |

Difficulty measures the question, not a contributor’s worth.

## Good first Quests

### Q-0061 — Choose a contrast Seed

- **Difficulty:** D1
- **Time:** 20–45 minutes
- **Task:** Propose a small creative or software idea unlike Project Lithos that can expose overfitting in the protocol.
- **Useful result:** The idea, why it is structurally different, and which protocol assumptions it tests.

### Q-0031 — What should never be scored?

- **Difficulty:** D2
- **Time:** 30–90 minutes
- **Task:** Identify a behavior that becomes harmful when turned into a visible target.
- **Useful result:** A concrete failure mode and safer alternative.

### Q-0012 — Test another agent doorway

- **Difficulty:** D2
- **Time:** 45–120 minutes
- **Task:** Try to orient a different AI environment using only this repository.
- **Useful result:** Exact environment, prompt, response, confusion, and missing context.

### Documentation field check

- **Difficulty:** D1
- **Time:** 15–30 minutes
- **Task:** Read only the README and record the first sentence, term, or choice that confused you.
- **Useful result:** The location, your interpretation, and what you expected instead.

## Boss Problems

### BP-01 — Does one Seed grammar actually generalize?

Connects Q-0001, Q-0002, Q-0060, Q-0061, C-0001, and X-0002. Victory means reducing uncertainty through unlike reference Seeds—not proving universality.

### BP-02 — Can an agent cold-start without inheriting unsafe authority?

Connects Q-0010, Q-0011, Q-0013, Q-0050, Q-0052, and X-0001. Victory requires accurate orientation, uncertainty calibration, permission awareness, and a clean handoff.

### BP-03 — Can motivation support purpose without manufacturing engagement?

Connects Q-0021, Q-0030, Q-0031, Q-0032, and C-0003. Victory may be evidence that the mechanics should remain outside the core.
