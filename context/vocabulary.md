# Vocabulary

## Canon

The currently accepted state used to coordinate work. Authoritative but challengeable; not synonymous with truth.

## Workspace

The area for drafts, hypotheses, branches, proposals, simulations, and other work that has not crossed an evidence or governance gate.

## Evidence

Material used to support, weaken, or contextualize a claim: sources, observations, calculations, simulations, experiments, or prototypes.

## History

Version changes, Decision rationale, provenance, failures, and rejected paths.

## Seed

A portable unit of human intent and structured knowledge that can be understood, challenged, extended, and handed onward without depending on the creator.

## Spark

A raw idea worth retaining but not yet coherent enough for independent handoff.

## Seed Protocol

The platform-independent rules and semantics defining a Seed and its interaction contract.

## Golden Seed

Seed S-0000: the self-hosting Seed that defines Seed Protocol.

## Seed Commons

A possible future human-and-agent platform built on Seed Protocol. It is not the protocol.

## Bootstrap

A compact orientation artifact that tells an unfamiliar participant how to enter the Seed, what state it is in, and where deeper context lives.

## Bootability

The ability of an unfamiliar agent to orient accurately and act usefully without prior conversational memory.

## Executability

The degree to which a Seed enables bounded machine research, simulation, experimentation, fabrication, or operation. Represented E0–E6.

## Maturity

The degree to which the idea has moved from raw thought toward researched and operational practice. Represented S0–S6.

## Decision

A recorded choice with context, alternatives, reasoning, consequences, evidence, and reversal conditions.

## Challenge

A durable attack on a claim, assumption, principle, Decision, model, implementation, incentive, or ontology.

## Experiment

A documented attempt to reduce uncertainty through a method that produces inspectable observations or artifacts.

## Derivative

An independently evolving Seed that declares what it inherited and where it departs.

## Contributor

Any human or agent that provides useful knowledge or work. Contribution does not imply membership or ongoing obligation.

## Steward

A participant responsible for coherence and Canon maintenance. Stewardship does not grant ownership of the conceptual future.

## Model boundary

An explicit account of what the Seed represents, excludes, cannot observe, and may be biased toward.

## Reversible Canon

The requirement that important accepted choices expose evidence or conditions that would cause review or reversal.

## Purpose matching

Connecting open questions to participants based on skills, interests, availability, location, and desired challenge.

## Agent role

A functional mode such as Scout, Skeptic, Archivist, or Experimentalist. A role is an assigned responsibility, not a claim about personhood.
