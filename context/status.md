## Current claims

- The concept is coherent enough to test.
- The file structure is provisional.
- The protocol has not demonstrated cross-domain generality.
- Agent bootability has only structural tests, not independent model evaluations.
- The repository is public and has explicit reuse licenses; long-term public governance remains unresolved.
- No Seed Commons product has been specified beyond conceptual direction.

## Highest risks

- The protocol may overfit long, interdisciplinary research concepts.
- Maintaining both narrative and structured representations may create drift.
- Participants may mistake a validated folder structure for validated knowledge.
- Challenge and Decision records may become bureaucracy.
- Agent-specific entry files may be misread as the platform-independent protocol.
- A public system may reward visible activity instead of truth-seeking.

## Blocked or awaiting human judgment
