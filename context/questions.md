# Open questions

Questions are contribution interfaces, not a generic backlog. Difficulty uses D1 (curiosity) through D5 (frontier). Status is open unless stated otherwise.

## Protocol core

### Q-0001 — What is the true minimum Seed?

- **Difficulty:** D3
- **Need:** information architecture, user research
- **Question:** Which artifacts are indispensable for transfer and cold start, and which are ceremony?
- **Evidence wanted:** comparison across at least three unlike Seeds.

### Q-0002 — Is one general Seed grammar viable?

- **Difficulty:** D5
- **Need:** cross-domain testing
- **Question:** Can engineering, creative, institutional, software, and research ideas share a small core without distortion?
- **Failure signal:** each domain requires incompatible semantics at the root.

### Q-0003 — How should Canon and Workspace promotion work?

- **Difficulty:** D4
- **Need:** governance, science practice, open-source maintenance
- **Question:** What minimum evidence and review are appropriate without imposing one governance model?

### Q-0004 — How much structure should confidence have?

- **Difficulty:** D4
- **Need:** epistemology, statistics, UX
- **Question:** Can uncertainty be explicit without fake numeric precision or exhausting authors?
