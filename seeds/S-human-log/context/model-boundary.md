# Model boundary

## Included

Human Log represents good-faith chief-of-staff memory for people and organizations: a current card, candidate claims, linked evidence, and a history of promotions, demotions, bounces, and corrections.

The represented chain is Observe (CRAWL and a CYA log) → Claim → Correlate → Grade → Promote. Entity types in view are Person, Organization, Foundation/Nonprofit, Role-at-org, and Household. Household is in view only for family logistics when that ask is made.

The represented operations cycle is a pre-meet stub, a purpose-scoped person-find, meet notes, a thank-you or follow-up that uses graded mail, then an update, then idle until the next trigger.

## Excluded or deferred

- Surveillance, harassment, and any use outside thank, greet, help, support, and the stated asks.
- Pretexting, unauthorized access, FCRA, GLBA, and DPPA data, Social Security numbers, and minors.
- Invented contact data, registered-agent addresses as mail destinations, and aggregator-only promotion.
- Group-channel copies of a private home or phone.
- The personal OneDrive OSINT pack itself. The design note says its ingest is done. This Seed does not hold that pack.
- Building confidence-grader, cya-log, the entity-card schema, or registry-ladder in this directory.
- A claim that HubSpot lead mail, a calendar invite, or a phone-contact entry has been reconciled with a card. Those connectors are gaps in the design note.

## Known biases

The origin is OSINT Academy / US-SOCOM Foundations training applied to one chief-of-staff practice, in English, by Jacob Nelson and Cord. That origin can overweight search procedure, registries, and grades, and underweight relationship memory and a short spontaneous thank-you. P-10 and the failure condition about spontaneity are the check on that bias.

The Seed file set can make the idea look more operational than it is. S1 means the concept is independently readable. It does not mean a card has been kept through a real meeting.

## Evidence quality

The v0.1 design note is the source for the thesis, grades, cycle, connector table, and software list. Distillation into this Seed is editorial. It is not a field trial.

No card, CYA log, bounce, or thank-you outcome is stored here. Connector "role today" sentences, including a completed ingest of the OSINT pack, are the note's statements and were not re-checked at planting. "Jacob greenlit" applies, in that note, to the four software efforts and to planting the note as a Seed.

## What would weaken the thesis

Revise, stop, or fork if the log becomes surveillance, if grades are ignored, if an aggregator is promoted alone, if the log is used to harass, or if the structure makes a sincere thank-you less likely than memory alone.

The thesis would also weaken if public lawful sources cannot support the courtesy mail the cycle depends on, or if two identity anchors still merge namesakes often enough that cards are unsafe to use.
