# Principles

These constraints govern Human Log more strongly than any store, connector, or tool. Each one changes a choice.

## P-01 — Good faith, bounded purpose

Use the log to thank, greet, help, and support. The asks it serves are thank-yous, business development, board and civic work, pre-meeting preparation, and family logistics when that logistics ask is made.

This choice stops a lookup that has no courtesy, help, or requested-logistics purpose. Surveillance and harassment are failures, not uses.

## P-02 — Public lawful sources

Research stays on public lawful sources. Pretexting and unauthorized access are out.

This choice stops a "likely address" that depends on a closed account, a pretext, or a non-public record.

## P-03 — Record the source or leave the field blank

Every contact or identity fact on a card has a source and an as-of date. Missing data stays missing.

This choice stops an agent from completing a thank-you address from recollection or pattern.

## P-04 — Two anchors before identity

Correlation requires at least two identity anchors and a namesake check before two mentions are treated as one person or organization.

This choice stops a shared name from inheriting someone else's mail.

## P-05 — Registered agent is not the mail destination

A registered-agent address stays off courtesy mail.

This choice stops a registry hit from being copied onto an envelope.

## P-06 — Official mail outranks a home street

For courtesy mail, the entity's official mail or PO Box is the destination ahead of a home street. The design note states the same rule for a foundation as PO Box over home street.

This choice changes which address is eligible for a thank-you when both exist.

## P-07 — Private home and phone stay one-to-one

A private home or phone is used only one-to-one with the owner. It is not written into a group channel.

This choice stops a card field from becoming a broadcast.

## P-08 — Sensitive classes stay out

FCRA, GLBA, and DPPA data, Social Security numbers, and minors are out of the log.

This choice ends a search or a card when the material falls in those classes, including a household record that would pull in a minor.

## P-09 — Grades gate the card

- **A** — registry or official source.
- **B** — the entity's own site.
- **C** — corroborated secondary. Two sources are required before promotion.
- **D** — aggregator. Needs Jacob's OK, and is never promoted alone.
- **X** — unresolved. Stays off the card.

Grade A or B, or grade C with two sources, may fill a Card field. Evidence stays linked. A bounce or a conflict demotes the field.

This choice stops an ungraded or aggregator-only value from becoming "current truth."

## P-10 — Structure serves the thank-you

The card, the log, and the grade exist so a thank-you or greeting can still happen. A procedure that crowds out a simple good-faith note is a failure condition of this Seed.

This choice stops a schema or a connector from becoming mandatory theater before a courtesy.
