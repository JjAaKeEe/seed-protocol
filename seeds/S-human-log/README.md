## One-sentence Seed

Human Log is an evidence-graded map of people and organizations so a chief of staff can thank, greet, help, and support them when human memory and agent memory are not enough.

## Why it may matter

Contact truth for courtesy and help is scattered and ungraded. A meet, a thank-you, or a request to find an address then depends on memory, an old thread, or an aggregator with no recorded source. Human Log keeps a current card only after a stated chain of observation, claim, correlation, and grade.
