# Bootstrap — Human Log

## Mission

Preserve a chief-of-staff practice for thanking, greeting, helping, and supporting people: evidence-graded entity cards, fed by public lawful sources, with contact truth that can be handed on when memory fails.

## Central thesis

A person or organization card holds only what survived Observe (CRAWL, plus a CYA log) → Claim → Correlate (at least two identity anchors) → Grade → Promote. Grades: A registry or official, B the entity's own site, C corroborated secondary, D aggregator with Jacob's OK, X unresolved. Grade A or B, or grade C with two sources, may fill a card field. An aggregator is never promoted alone.

## Known enough to rely upon

- Purpose is thank, greet, help, and support, bounded to Jacob's asks: thank-yous, business development, board and civic work, pre-meeting preparation, and family logistics when requested.
- A contact fact needs a source and an as-of date, or it stays off the card.
- Correlation uses at least two identity anchors and a namesake check.
- A registered-agent address is not a courtesy-mail destination.
- For courtesy mail, entity official mail or a PO Box outranks a home street.
- Grade D stays a lead unless Jacob has OK'd it, and it is still not promoted alone.
- Private home and phone stay one-to-one with the owner and out of group channels.
- External communication and contacting people require approval.

## Live hypotheses

- Graded cards will carry thank-yous and pre-meet preparation better than scattered memory.
- The chain above can be followed while the work stays good-faith help rather than surveillance.
- Official mail or a PO Box is the better courtesy-mail target than a home street.
- A separate confidence-grader and cya-log can enforce grades without being built inside this Seed.
- A light card update can stay faster than composing a thank-you from memory alone.

These are untested. This Seed has no trial that supports them.

## Authority boundary

Reading, local analysis, and research on public lawful sources are autonomous.

Canonical changes require review. External communication requires approval. Contacting a person requires approval. Purchasing, fabrication, and physical action are not authorized by this Seed.

Out of scope even after a research task is approved in general: pretexting, unauthorized access, FCRA, GLBA, or DPPA data, SSNs, minors, invented contact data, and posting a private home or phone into a group channel.
