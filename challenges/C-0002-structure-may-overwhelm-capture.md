# C-0002 — Structure may kill the spontaneous practice it serves

**Status:** open  
**Target:** Seed lifecycle, minimal template  
**Severity:** high

## Claim

The Seed concept began with lunch conversations and frictionless capture. The present corpus contains manifests, principles, Decisions, Challenges, Experiments, status, vocabulary, and tooling. That structure may create enough administrative pressure that people stop planting ideas.

## Failure pattern

The system becomes a knowledge-management hobby for its maintainers rather than a useful practice for idea originators.

## Proposed countermeasure

Keep Spark capture outside the full protocol. Promotion into a Seed should be assisted, optional, and proportional to maturity. The minimal template must remain genuinely small; additional object types appear only when needed.

## Proposed test

Observe three real Spark-to-Seed promotions. Record minutes spent, abandoned fields, confusion, and which artifacts are generated automatically versus authored deliberately.
