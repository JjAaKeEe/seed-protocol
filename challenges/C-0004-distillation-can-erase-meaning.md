# C-0004 — Distillation can erase the meaning it intends to preserve

**Status:** open  
**Target:** context compression, Canon  
**Severity:** foundational

## Claim

Turning long conversations into concise canonical state can remove uncertainty, disagreement, emotional motivation, tacit context, minority contributions, and the strange associative path that made the idea original.

## Risk

The Seed may become easier for agents to consume while becoming less faithful to the human intent that justified it.

## Proposed mitigations

- preserve provenance and selected source excerpts privately where appropriate;
- record disputed interpretations;
- distinguish summary from quotation and observation;
- have originators review major distillations;
- retain a model-boundary section describing likely loss.

## Proposed test

Ask originators and uninvolved readers separately to explain the idea from the Seed. Compare fidelity, omissions, and unintended reframing.
