# SPARK.md: draft spec (working draft, not frozen)

A Spark is the smallest capture of an idea, a protocol object. Frozen only after three real Spark-to-Seed promotions are observed (see D-0009).

`SPARK.md` is 5 lines or fewer:
1. Idea (one sentence)
2. Who it is for / why it matters
3. What would prove it wrong
4. Source and date
5. Authority: who may act on it (default: none)

Promotion to a Seed: copy to a new Seed from `templates/` and expand. No tooling exists yet; the validator does not check Sparks.
