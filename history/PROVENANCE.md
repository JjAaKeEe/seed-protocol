# Provenance

## Origin

Seed S-0000 emerged from a human-led ChatGPT conversation on 2026-09-20. The conversation began with Project Lithos, a speculative framework for regenerative subterranean human habitation, then expanded into the problem of preserving ambitious ideas for other humans and intelligent systems.

The originating human described a recurring experience: interesting ideas form during lunches and conversations, but neither execution nor disappearance is an adequate default. GitHub was proposed as a practical starting surface because versioning, forks, issues, and attribution already exist. The discussion then separated the portable Seed object from any particular platform.

Subsequent turns introduced:

- human-readable and agent-bootable duality;
- Seed Protocol versus Seed Commons;
- deterministic bootstrapping;
- maturity and executability as separate dimensions;
- explicit authority boundaries for tool-using agents;
- Challenges as first-class attacks on a Seed;
- motivation, purpose matching, and non-exploitative game mechanics;
- red teaming, antifragility, assumed incompleteness, and ontological challenge;
- the Golden Seed as a self-hosting “repo of the repo.”

The human then requested a detailed standalone package capable of surviving the loss of the original chat and intended to continue the work in Codex. This repository is the first implementation of that handoff.

The owner placed a 147-section constitutional shell into the working directory as `seed_golden_repo.md`. The outline contains substantial originating material but was always intended to be populated and built out completely with human and agent collaboration. Its planted version is preserved by the initial Git commit. During R&D it may be rewritten, expanded, illustrated, reorganized, split, or extended with new sections.

- `seed_golden_repo.md` is the expanding constitutional corpus and preserves the source-of-origin through its history.
- `SEED.md` expresses the current distilled Canon.
- `spec/seed-protocol-v0.1-draft.md` expresses testable normative behavior.

If the distilled Canon appears to lose a value, distinction, or unresolved tension, the constitutional shell and its Git history are evidence for opening a Challenge or Decision—not a reason to rewrite history invisibly.

## Synthesis

- **Human originator:** supplied the central intent, use cases, corrections, values, and creative direction.
- **ChatGPT:** developed and organized the initial conceptual vocabulary during the referenced conversation.
- **Codex:** distilled the conversation into this self-describing repository, reference specification, template, tooling, tests, Decisions, Challenges, and Experiments.

The AI contributions are synthesis and implementation work, not evidence that the protocol's claims are true.

## Source handling

The original conversation is source material, not Canon. It may contain unverified facts, obsolete references, private context, repetition, and exploratory statements. This corpus preserves the conceptual state needed to continue while deliberately omitting most conversational detail.

The constitutional shell is both source material and active R&D territory. It is authoritative about origin and intent only where explicitly stated; its structure, prose, diagrams, proposals, and omissions remain challengeable.
